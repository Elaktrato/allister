Backup

{{if .Error}}

{{.Error}}

{{end}}

The backup is one .zip file with every item of every plugin (also of disabled plugins): titles, fields, genres and tags, external ids, relations, credits, your list entries with ratings, comments, dates and progress, and the local cover images.

Your appearance settings and uploaded fonts are included too. Other settings, plugin choices, background jobs and import history are not included. Person photos are not included either: they are downloaded again after a restore.

Restore

Restoring adds the items of a backup that are not in your library yet. Items you already have are skipped and never overwritten, and nothing is deleted. Restored items are synced again in the background. Uploaded fonts are added; the appearance settings are restored only when this installation has none of its own.
