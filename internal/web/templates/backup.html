The backup is one .zip file with every item of every plugin (also of disabled plugins): titles, fields, genres and tags, external ids, relations, credits, your list entries with ratings, comments, dates and progress, and the local cover images.

Settings, plugin choices, background jobs and import history are not included. Person photos are not included either: they are downloaded again after a restore.
